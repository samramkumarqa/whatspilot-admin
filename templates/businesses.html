ℹ️ What happens after I add a business?

A new business starts Inactive - it's registered, but automation rules won't run for it and its owner won't be able to sign in to their business portal yet.

Click Activate on the business below when it's ready to go live. By default the owner logs in with the Business WhatsApp Number above - no separate personal number needed - and their login code arrives there by SMS.
